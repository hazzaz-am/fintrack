## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Auth screens remain usable at phone widths
The login and register forms SHALL remain fully usable at phone widths, centered and legible without horizontal scrolling or oversized/clipped elements.

#### Scenario: Login/register screen viewed at 375px width
- **WHEN** the login or register screen is loaded at a 375px viewport width
- **THEN** the form fields, labels, and submit action remain fully visible and operable without horizontal scrolling, and on-screen keyboard focus does not push the submit action off-screen
