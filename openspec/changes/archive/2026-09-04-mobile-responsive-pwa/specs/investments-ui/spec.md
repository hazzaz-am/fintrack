## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Investments screen remains usable at phone widths
The investment card/list layout and the create/contribute/record-maturity forms SHALL remain fully usable at phone widths without horizontal scrolling or clipped figures.

#### Scenario: Investments screen viewed at 375px width
- **WHEN** the Investments screen is loaded at a 375px viewport width
- **THEN** investment cards stack in a single column with all figures (principal, expected return, maturity date) visible without clipping, and the contribute/record-maturity forms present as single-column
