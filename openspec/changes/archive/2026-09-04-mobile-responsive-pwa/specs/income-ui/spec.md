## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Income screen remains usable at phone widths
The income list and its filter/entry forms SHALL remain fully usable at phone widths without horizontal scrolling or clipped amounts.

#### Scenario: Income screen viewed at 375px width
- **WHEN** the Income screen is loaded at a 375px viewport width
- **THEN** the income list rows and any filter controls remain fully visible and operable without horizontal scrolling
